Pizza

Description

Pizza is a savory Italian dish made of a flattened base of leavened wheat dough, typically topped with tomato sauce, cheese (such as mozzarella), and various other ingredients like meats, vegetables, and herbs, and baked at a high temperature.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Olive Oil
  • Tomatoes
  • Seasonings
  • Mozarella
  • Hard Cheese
  • vegetables

Steps

  1. Make and rise the dough
  2. Prep the oven and sauce
  3. Shape and top the pizza
  4. Bake and serve
  5. Serve
