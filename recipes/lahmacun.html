Lahmacun

Description

Lahmacun is a thin, crispy Turkish flatbread topped with a savory spiced minced meat and vegetable mixture.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Olive Oil
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Minced Meat
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Peppers
  • Parsley
  • Cumin
  • paprika
  • Black Pepper
  • Red Pepper Flakes

Steps

  1. Make the dough
  2. Prepare the meat topping
  3. Shape the Lahmacun
  4. Cook
  5. Serve
